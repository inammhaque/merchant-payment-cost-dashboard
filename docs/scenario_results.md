# Scenario results

Shift of 20% of card (credit + debit) transactions to e-Transfer, at e-Transfer fees of $0.00, $0.50, $1.00, $1.50 per transaction, under both pricing models. Generated by `data/scenario_report.py` from `/api/scenario`; approved transactions only, no filters.

**Method**

- Shift is proportional and deterministic: fees avoided = 20% x card fees; e-Transfer cost = 20% x card transactions x e-Transfer fee; savings = fees avoided - e-Transfer cost.
- Current fees include existing e-Transfer volume at its current fee ($0.00); the scenario fee applies only to the shifted transactions.
- Break-even ticket = (e-Transfer fee - card flat fee) / card percentage fee. e-Transfer is cheaper for tickets above it. "all sizes" means e-Transfer is cheaper at every ticket size.
- Category values are rounded to cents individually, so their sums can differ from the totals by a cent.
- The interchange model covers interchange only (no network assessments or processor markup). Debit rates in that model reuse the Square flat-rate figures (see `data/fee_assumptions.csv`).
